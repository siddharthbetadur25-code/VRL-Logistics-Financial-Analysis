 VRL Logistics – Financial Performance Analysis

📊 Project Overview

This project presents a five-year financial performance analysis of **VRL Logistics Ltd.** for the period **2020–2021 to 2024–2025**.

The study evaluates the company's financial position and performance using financial ratio analysis, comparative financial statement analysis and trend analysis. The analysis focuses on liquidity, profitability, solvency and asset utilization.

The project was completed as part of my MBA Finance academic project.

🎯 Objectives

* To analyse the financial performance of VRL Logistics Ltd.
* To evaluate the company's short-term liquidity position.
* To analyse profitability and returns.
* To examine the company's solvency and capital structure.
* To evaluate the efficiency of asset utilization.
* To identify major financial trends and areas requiring improvement.

🔍 Key Findings

 1. Liquidity

The Current Ratio remained below the generally accepted benchmark of 2:1 during the study period, indicating pressure on the company's short-term liquidity and working capital position.

The Quick Ratio also remained below the 1:1 benchmark throughout the period, although it improved in 2024–2025 compared with the previous year.

 2. Profitability

The Net Profit Ratio fluctuated during the study period. It increased significantly in 2021–2022, declined in 2023–2024 and recovered in 2024–2025.

 3. Solvency

The Debt–Equity Ratio remained below 1 during the study period, indicating a relatively stable capital structure and moderate financial risk. However, the increasing ratio in recent years indicates greater dependence on borrowings for expansion.

 4. Asset Utilization

The Asset Turnover Ratio improved to 1.73 in 2021–2022 but subsequently declined to 1.22 by 2024–2025. This indicates that the company's asset base was growing faster than revenue, resulting in lower asset utilization efficiency.

 5. Revenue Growth

Revenue increased consistently during the study period, indicating expansion in the company's operations. However, the analysis also highlights the need to improve liquidity management and asset utilization.

---

 📌 Overall Conclusion

The analysis indicates that VRL Logistics Ltd. maintained a relatively stable financial base with moderate profitability and controlled financial risk during the study period.

However, the company needs to focus on **improving short-term liquidity, strengthening working capital management and improving asset utilization efficiency** to support sustainable long-term financial performance.
